# Layover logo — four directions

Each mark was derived from a random 64-char string; the direction comes from
structure found inside the string, not from the surface of it.

## A — "Ground time"
    MiDwdpgr8HtzCgSqBOcuzUnoqzNldFJP74ST1SqPN0eRRcgWsg2PebtJGj4kNPBe
Digits, in order: **8 7 4 1 0 2 4**. A monotone descent to zero, then a climb
back out. Only one adjacent repeat in 64 characters (`RR`) — the string is
almost entirely motion, with a single held beat. That is a layover: a long
descent, a stop at zero, a shorter and steeper departure.
Mark: one continuous path that falls, flattens, and leaves — with a marigold
dot at rest in the trough. The dot is the zero. It is the moment you're in.

## B — "The meeting"
    y0ywZlkQvSb4L5jz5sr6ZJXGg0ZCX89g2iRK2OMmh88tyPWUu8k6FFjrmHLpqWtV
Everything in this string arrives in twos: `Gg` `Mm` `Uu` `FF` `88`, and the
digit 8 (itself two closed loops) appears five times — more than any other.
The recurring figure is the same thing twice, side by side.
Mark: two open rings, each complete on its own, touching at exactly one point.
One is you, one is the agent; the tangent is the handoff. Nothing overlaps,
nothing merges — they meet, and go on.

## C — "The fold"
    N9PIge2EKQYHiiXEgA7LESkbOXvuPsbFbqLlMYURussp42GxZfkmgAuzoA4usgWz
Buried at position 41 is `Ll` — the letter of Layover, in both cases, adjacent.
The string's other repeats (`ii`, `ss`) are the same doubling.
Mark: the L twice, the second rotated 180 degrees into the first, woven so
neither is on top. Two overshooting tails keep it from closing into a plain
square. Meaning: the workspace and you, folded together.

## D — "The held gap"
    G5X44bF8P5N7FoXCyzTwWSASjE5UxxjPsJgF6lgPOdCfb9tun2ODZSDclRx04ccH
`44` `xx` `cc` `wW`, and `SAS` at the center — mirrors, and a palindrome that
turns on an axis. What matters in a mirror is not either side; it is the line
between them.
Mark: two brackets facing each other, holding a lens of empty space. The logo
is the gap. Layover is the time in between, so let the negative space be the
subject and the strokes be the thing that holds it open.
