Layover — color, type, space, elevation, motion

A calm workspace for the time in between.

Layover is where you keep a project's thinking between sessions. An agent can push updates into it while it works, but the app is not a monitor — it's a place you'd open on your own. Everything here is built on a 6px unit, a seven-step type scale, and round-cornered surfaces that stay quiet until you touch them.

1

The room stays still

Incoming updates never move what you're reading or steal focus. They arrive, they wait, they hold their place until you look.

2

Workspaces are people-shaped

A workspace is a place with a color and a name, not a file path. Switching is one click and never loses your place in the last one.

3

Nothing urgent is bright

Marigold means something is happening elsewhere. It's the only warm color in the interface, and it never asks to be clicked.

Foundations — Color

Warm stone, one cool accent, one warm signal

Neutrals carry a slight green bias so they sit with teal rather than under it. Teal is the only interactive color. Marigold is reserved entirely for work happening somewhere else — it is never a button.

Foundations — Typography

Seven steps, two voices

Familjen Grotesk sets anything that names a thing — titles, workspace names, card headings. Figtree carries everything you read at length; its round bowls are why the interface feels soft at 13px. Mono appears only for token names and counts, never for prose.

Foundations — Space & radius

Everything lands on six

One unit, seven stops. Gaps inside a component use s1–s3; gaps between components use s3–s5; s6 and s7 separate whole regions. Radius scales with the size of the surface, so a chip and a modal feel like the same family.

Foundations — Elevation

Four levels, and most things are level zero

Depth is spent on things that genuinely float. Cards sit flat and gain a shadow only on hover; only menus, modals and toasts get e3. In dark mode shadows do almost nothing, so each level also raises the surface color — the border does the work the shadow can't.

--e0Flat. List rows, fields, most cards at rest.
--e1Barely lifted. Segmented control thumb.
--e2Hover and drag. Cards you're touching.
--e3Floating. Menus, modals, toasts.
Foundations — Motion

Three durations. No bounce, ever.

Motion confirms a change; it doesn't perform one. Anything the user initiated resolves in quick. Anything that arrives on its own uses calm, so it registers without pulling the eye. Slow belongs to the breathing indicators and nothing else.

--dur-quick · 120ms Press, hover, toggle. The user is already looking at it.
--dur-calm · 240ms An update arrives, a card lands, a panel opens.
--dur-slow · 480ms Breathing states and the rest timer. Loops, never fires once.

Every duration halves under prefers-reduced-motion, and the two looping animations stop entirely.
